Tell local shops and service providers what you want and let them compete for your order. I Want India is the reverse marketplace for getting quotes: instead of visiting ten shops or calling five technicians, you post one request and sellers near you send their best price.

HOW IT WORKS
• Post a request in under a minute. Type or speak what you need ("AC service, 2 split ACs", "Samsung 300L double-door fridge delivered to 560034 by Friday", "100 printed T-shirts"), add photos, your PIN code, a budget and a date.
• Get quotes. Local shops, technicians and online sellers send itemised quotes with price, GST (CGST/SGST or IGST), delivery, installation, warranty and dates.
• Compare quotes side by side. Sort by price, rating, delivery date or distance.
• Chat, bargain with a counter-offer and accept the best quote. Your address and phone number are shared only with the seller you choose.

FOR SHOPS AND SERVICE PROVIDERS
Get new customers without paying for ads. Choose your categories and service area by PIN code or distance, see matching requests in your lead feed and send a quote in seconds with saved templates.
