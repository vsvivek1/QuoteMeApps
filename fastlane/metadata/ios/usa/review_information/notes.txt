USER-GENERATED CONTENT (Guideline 1.2): every chat, request, quote, review and profile has Report and Block in its "..." menu. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours; images are screened automatically.

ACCOUNT DELETION: Settings > Delete account (re-authentication required; Apple token is revoked for Sign in with Apple users).

PURCHASES: seller plans and quote credits use StoreKit; they are hidden while our launch promotion keeps seller features free. Sign in with Apple is offered alongside Google sign-in.

Payments for goods and services happen directly between buyers and sellers outside the app (physical goods and real-world services).

Language: English (Spanish in Settings > Language).
