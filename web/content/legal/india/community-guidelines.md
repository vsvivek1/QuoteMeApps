# Community Guidelines and Prohibited and Restricted Items

_Version 1.0.0 · Last updated 2026-10-02_

I Want India works when buyers post real needs and sellers send honest quotes. These guidelines apply to everything on the Service: requests, quotes, chat, photos, profiles and reviews. They form part of our [Terms of Use](/legal/terms-of-service) (including the content rules in Section 7, which follow Rule 3(1)(b) of the IT Rules, 2021) and our [Seller Terms](/legal/seller-terms).

## 1. Be honest and respectful

- Post genuine requests and quotes. No fake, duplicate or test requests, bait prices or quotes for items you cannot supply.
- Treat everyone with respect. No harassment, threats, bullying, or content that is insulting on the basis of gender, religion, caste, race, ethnicity, region, language or disability, or that promotes enmity between groups.
- No obscene, pornographic or sexually explicit content, adult services, or anything harmful to children.
- No violent, graphic or shocking content.
- No spam, chain messages, unrelated advertising or scams (for example advance-payment fraud, fake UPI "collect" requests or QR codes sent to receive money).
- Do not share other people's personal information. Never ask buyers for OTPs, UPI PINs, card details or bank logins.
- Do not impersonate any person or business, or misrepresent your registrations, ratings or affiliation.
- Do not post content that infringes intellectual property, including counterfeit or "first copy" goods.

## 2. Allowed, restricted and blocked categories

Every category has one of three states:

- **Allowed**: anyone can request and any verified seller can quote.
- **Restricted (registered sellers only)**: buyers can request, but only sellers who upload and pass review of the required registration can see or quote these requests. The registration type, number, issuing body and expiry are stored and re-checked before expiry. Request and quote screens show the regulatory disclaimer for the category.
- **Blocked**: cannot be requested or quoted at all. Our text classifier also catches free-text requests that try to get around this.

When we are unsure whether a category is lawful to offer, it starts as **Blocked**. The current policy for I Want India is:

## 3. Always prohibited

Regardless of category, you may not request, offer or quote for:

- Arms, ammunition, explosives and weapons, including items requiring a licence under the Arms Act, 1959.
- Narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances (NDPS Act, 1985), prescription and over-the-counter medicines, and drug paraphernalia.
- Alcohol, tobacco products, e-cigarettes and vapes.
- Counterfeit, "first copy", replica or stolen goods, and goods that infringe a trademark, copyright or patent.
- Adult or sexual services, escort services, and any content sexualising children.
- Loans, credit, chit funds, investments, securities, cryptocurrency, foreign exchange and money transfer services.
- Betting, gambling, lotteries and online money games.
- Wildlife and products protected under the Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972; human organs and body parts.
- Goods banned or restricted by Indian law, hazardous chemicals, and products sold without a mandatory BIS certification.
- Fake documents, identity documents (including Aadhaar or PAN services), hacking services, personal data or accounts.
- Services that would require you to break the law, including unregistered regulated services and legal services by advocates.

## 4. Regulatory disclaimers

These disclaimers are shown on request and quote screens in the categories they apply to. If insurance is ever enabled, the IRDAI wording below is shown on every insurance request and quote together with the registration number of the IRDAI-registered entity.

- **Insurance (health, motor, life, home):** Insurance is the subject matter of solicitation. Calecute Technologies (OPC) Private Limited is not an insurer, insurance intermediary or web aggregator registered with IRDAI and does not solicit or sell insurance. Quotes are provided by the IRDAI-registered entity named on the quote (IRDAI Reg. No. <shown on each quote>). For details on risk factors, terms, conditions and exclusions, read the sales brochure carefully before concluding a sale.
- **Real estate sale and rental:** Verify the RERA registration of the project and agent on the State RERA website before making any payment. Calecute Technologies (OPC) Private Limited is not a real estate agent and is not party to any property transaction.
- **Medical practitioners, doctors and clinics:** I Want India does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Do not share detailed health information in requests or chat. In an emergency call 112.
- **Contractors (electrical, plumbing, AC, civil):** Ask your contractor for the relevant licence or certificate for electrical and gas work.
- **Packers and movers:** Ask for a written lorry receipt / goods consignment note and transit insurance details before your goods are loaded.
- **Used vehicles:** Check the RC, insurance, PUC certificate and challan history before you pay, and complete the RTO ownership transfer.
- **Everyday goods (appliances, electronics, furniture) and home services:** Prices include GST unless the quote says otherwise. Ask for a GST invoice.

## 5. Reviews

Reviews must reflect a real, completed order. No paid, incentivised, swapped or self-written reviews, and no threats to leave a bad review to get a discount. Sellers may reply once, politely.

## 6. Enforcement and grievances

You can **report** and **block** any user, request, quote, chat or review. Content with several reports may be hidden automatically while we review it. Uploaded photos are screened automatically for unsafe content. Depending on severity we may remove or disable access to content, issue a warning, limit features, remove a Verified badge, or suspend or terminate the account, and we may report unlawful content to the authorities as required by law. If we remove your content we will tell you why, and you can ask our [Grievance Officer](/legal/grievance-officer) to review the decision.

If you see something that puts someone in immediate danger, call 112.
