# Refund and Cancellation Policy

_Version 1.0.0 · Last updated 2026-10-02_

This policy covers payments made **to Calecute Technologies (OPC) Private Limited** for I Want India seller subscriptions and quote-credit packs. Buyers use I Want India for free.

**Purchases between buyers and sellers are not covered.** Buyers pay sellers directly (UPI, cash or card), outside the Service, so refunds, returns, cancellations and warranty claims for goods or services must be agreed with the seller under the seller's own policy and the Consumer Protection Act, 2019. You can report a seller who refuses to honour an accepted quote or raise a complaint with our [Grievance Officer](/legal/grievance-officer), and we may act against the seller under our [Community Guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines).

## 1. Where you bought determines who handles it

| Where you paid | How to cancel | How to ask for a refund |
|---|---|---|
| Google Play (Android app) | Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, or the "Manage subscription" link in the app | Google handles refunds under the Google Play refund policy. Within 48 hours of purchase you can request one at play.google.com; after that, contact us and we will review it under Section 3 |
| Apple App Store (iOS app) | iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions, or the "Manage subscription" link in the app | Apple handles all refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds for App Store purchases |
| Our website (seller dashboard, paid with Razorpay) | Seller dashboard > Billing > Cancel plan. For UPI AutoPay you can also revoke the mandate in your UPI app; for card mandates, through your bank | Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}; we review it under Section 3 |

## 2. Cancelling a subscription

- You can cancel at any time. Cancellation stops the next renewal and cancels the recurring mandate; you keep your plan benefits until the end of the period you already paid for.
- Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription. Cancel it in the store first. Deleting your I Want India account cancels website subscriptions and their mandates automatically.
- If you cancel during a free trial, you will not be charged. Apple and Google may require you to cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

## 3. Refunds for website purchases

- **Subscriptions.** Payments for a started billing period are non-refundable, except where required by law, for duplicate or mistaken debits, or if the Service was unavailable for a significant part of the period because of a fault on our side. We do not give partial refunds for unused time after cancellation.
- **Annual plans.** If you cancel an annual plan within 14 days of the initial purchase (not a renewal), we refund it in full.
- **Quote-credit packs.** Unused credit packs can be refunded within 14 days of purchase if no credits from the pack have been used. Credits expire 12 months after purchase.
- **Failed transactions.** If money was debited but the purchase failed, it is reversed automatically by Razorpay or your bank, usually within 5 to 7 working days. Contact us if it is not.
- **Account suspension.** If we close your seller account because you broke our Terms, fees already paid are not refunded. If we close it without cause, we refund the unused part of any prepaid period.

## 4. How refunds are paid

Refund requests are answered within 2 working days. Approved refunds are initiated within 7 working days and credited to the original payment method (card, UPI or bank account) through Razorpay; banks usually take a further 5 to 7 working days to show the credit. We issue a GST credit note for every refund. Any unused credits or plan benefits linked to the refunded purchase are removed.
