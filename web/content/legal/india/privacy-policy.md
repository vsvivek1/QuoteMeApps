# Privacy Policy

_Version 1.0.0 · Last updated 2026-10-02_

This Privacy Policy explains how Calecute Technologies (OPC) Private Limited (CIN {{CIN}}) ("Calecute Technologies (OPC) Private Limited", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the I Want India mobile app for Android and iOS, the website at https://{{INDIA_WEB_DOMAIN}} and the seller dashboard at https://{{INDIA_SELLER_DASHBOARD_DOMAIN}} (together, the "Service").

We are the **Data Fiduciary** for your personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act") and the rules made under it, and this policy is also published under the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 (the "IT Rules").

I Want India is a reverse marketplace for India: buyers post what they want and independent sellers send quotes. I Want India is a separate app from "I Want USA"; the two apps share no accounts and no data.

Read this policy together with our [DPDP Consent Notice](/legal/dpdp-consent-notice), which is shown to you when you sign up, and our [Grievance Officer](/legal/grievance-officer) page.

## 1. Summary

- We process only the personal data needed to run the marketplace, on the basis of **your consent** (which you can withdraw at any time) and the legitimate uses allowed by the DPDP Act.
- We **do not sell** your personal data. The app shows **no ads**, does **not track** you across other apps or websites, and does not use the Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA.
- Your data is stored by our database provider Supabase in **AWS ap-south-1 (Mumbai, India)**.
- A buyer's exact address and phone number are shown to a seller **only after the buyer accepts that seller's quote**.
- You can delete your account at any time in **Settings > Delete account** or at [https://{{INDIA_WEB_DOMAIN}}/legal/account-deletion](/legal/account-deletion).

## 3. How and why we use personal data

We process personal data on the basis of your consent given through the [DPDP Consent Notice](/legal/dpdp-consent-notice), and for legitimate uses permitted by Section 7 of the DPDP Act (for example where you voluntarily provide data for a specific purpose, or to comply with law or a court order). We use it to:

1. Create and secure your account and sign you in (phone one-time code, Google or Apple).
2. Run the marketplace: post requests, match them to sellers by category and service area (PIN code or radius), deliver quotes, chat, accept quotes, track orders and publish reviews.
3. Verify sellers (GSTIN, shop photo, optional Udyam number), check licences for restricted categories and show "Verified" badges.
4. Send notifications you asked for (new quotes, messages, order updates) and service messages. Promotional messages by SMS, WhatsApp or email are sent only if you opt in, through DLT-registered templates where required, and you can opt out at any time.
5. Keep the Service safe: detect fake or duplicate requests, spam, scams and abuse; enforce our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service) and [Community Guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines); handle reports, blocks and grievances; moderate uploaded photos.
6. Process seller plan and quote-credit purchases and issue GST invoices.
7. Analyze usage (only if you opt in) and fix crashes and bugs.
8. Comply with Indian law, including the IT Rules and directions of CERT-In, and respond to lawful requests from authorities.

We do not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects. Seller ranking in quote lists is explained in our [Seller Terms](/legal/seller-terms).

## 4. What other users see

- **Sellers matching your request** see its category, description, photos, quantity, needed-by date, your budget (unless you hide it), the locality or PIN code (not the exact address) and your display name.
- **After you accept a quote**, that seller also sees your exact address and phone number so they can deliver or provide the service. Before acceptance, contact happens only through in-app chat.
- **Seller profiles** (business name, photos, description, categories, service area, rating, reviews, verification badge, GSTIN and licence numbers for restricted categories) are public.
- **Reviews** you write are public with your display name.

## 7. Your rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

- **Access** a summary of the personal data we process about you and the processing activities, and the identities of the Data Fiduciaries and Data Processors with whom it has been shared (Settings > Privacy > Download my data, or email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}).
- **Correction, completion and updating** of inaccurate or incomplete data (edit your profile in the app or email us).
- **Erasure** of your data (Settings > Delete account, or the [Account Deletion page](/legal/account-deletion)), except data we must keep by law.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, as easily as you gave it: Settings > Privacy > Manage consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done. If you withdraw consent needed to run your account, we will close it.
- **Grievance redressal**: contact our [Grievance Officer](/legal/grievance-officer). If you are not satisfied with our response you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
- **Nominate** another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity (Settings > Privacy > Nominee, or email us).

We will respond to rights requests within 30 days, and to grievances within the timelines on the [Grievance Officer](/legal/grievance-officer) page. We may verify your identity by confirming control of your registered phone number.

You may also manage your consent through a Consent Manager registered with the Data Protection Board once such services are available.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable security practices and procedures in line with the IT Act and the DPDP Act: encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, row-level security rules so users can only read the data they are allowed to see, private storage for verification documents, least-privilege staff access with logging, and regular review of our safeguards. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform affected users and the Data Protection Board of India, and report cyber incidents to CERT-In, within the timelines the law prescribes.
