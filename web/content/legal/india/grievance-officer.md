## What you can raise

- Content or conduct on I Want India that violates our [Terms of Use](/legal/terms-of-service) (including the content rules in Section 7) or [Community Guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines)
- Complaints about a seller, a quote or an order, including consumer complaints under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020
- Objections to our action on your content or account (removal, suspension)
- Requests and complaints about your personal data, including access, correction, erasure and consent withdrawal
- Intellectual property complaints

## How to file a grievance

1. In the app: **Settings > Help and support > Raise a grievance**, or
2. Email {{GRIEVANCE_EMAIL}} with the subject "Grievance".

Include your name, registered mobile number, a description of the issue, links or screenshots of the content (request, quote, chat, profile or review), and what you would like us to do. You will receive a ticket number.

## Timelines

| Step | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | Within 24 hours |
| Resolution of a complaint | Within 15 days of receipt |
| Request to remove content that is prohibited under Rule 3(1)(b) of the IT Rules | Acted on within 72 hours of the complaint |
| Content that exposes a person's private area, shows full or partial nudity or sexual acts, or impersonates a person (including morphed images) | Removed or access disabled within 24 hours of the complaint |
| Personal data rights requests under the DPDP Act | Within 30 days, and not later than the period prescribed by the DPDP Rules |

If you are not satisfied with the resolution, you may appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee constituted under the IT Rules (https://gac.gov.in) within 30 days of our decision, complain to the Data Protection Board of India about personal data matters, or approach the National Consumer Helpline (1915) or a Consumer Commission for consumer complaints.
