# Cookie Policy

_Version 1.0.0 · Last updated 2026-10-02_

This policy explains how Calecute Technologies LLC uses cookies and similar technologies (such as browser local storage) on the I Want USA websites: https://{{USA_WEB_DOMAIN}} (including the legal, request and seller pages), the seller dashboard at https://{{USA_SELLER_DASHBOARD_DOMAIN}} and our admin panel. The mobile app does not use browser cookies; see the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy) for what the app collects.

## 1. Cookies we use

| Name or type | Category | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sb-<project>-auth-token` (local storage or cookie) | Strictly necessary | Keeps you signed in to the seller dashboard and admin panel (Supabase Auth session) | Until you sign out; refresh token up to 30 days |
| `iwant_consent` | Strictly necessary | Remembers your cookie choices | 12 months |
| `iwant_locale` | Functional | Remembers your language | 12 months |
| Cloudflare Turnstile | Strictly necessary | Bot protection on sign-up, waitlist, contact and report forms | Session |
| `__stripe_mid`, `__stripe_sid` or Razorpay checkout cookies (only on payment pages) | Strictly necessary | Fraud prevention during seller plan checkout by our payment provider (Stripe) | Up to 1 year / 30 minutes |
| `_vercel_*` (preview deployments only) | Strictly necessary | Access protection for preview sites, not used on the live site | Session |
| Vercel Web Analytics | Analytics (cookieless) | Counts page views in aggregate without cookies or cross-site identifiers | Not stored on your device |

We **do not** use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels or social media tracking cookies, and we do not allow third parties to place advertising cookies on our sites.

## 2. Your choices

Strictly necessary cookies are needed for sign-in, security and payments and cannot be switched off in our cookie settings, but you can block them in your browser (some features will then stop working). Functional cookies can be turned off in **Cookie settings** in the site footer. You can also set your browser to send the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal; we treat it as an opt-out of sale and sharing (we do not sell or share personal information in any case).
