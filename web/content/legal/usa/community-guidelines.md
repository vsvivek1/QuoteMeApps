# Community Guidelines and Prohibited and Restricted Items

_Version 1.0.0 · Last updated 2026-10-02_

I Want USA works when buyers post real needs and sellers send honest quotes. These guidelines apply to everything on the Service: requests, quotes, chat, photos, profiles and reviews. They form part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service) and [Seller Terms](/legal/seller-terms).

## 1. Be honest and respectful

- Post genuine requests and quotes. No fake, duplicate or test requests, bait prices or quotes for items you cannot supply.
- Treat everyone with respect. No harassment, threats, bullying, hate speech, or discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, disability, age, familial status or any other protected characteristic.
- No sexual or sexually suggestive content, nudity, adult services, or content involving minors in any sexual or harmful way.
- No violent, graphic or shocking content.
- No spam, chain messages, unrelated advertising, link farming or scams (for example advance-fee requests, gift-card payments or fake overpayment checks).
- Do not share other people's personal information. Do not ask buyers for passwords, one-time codes or bank logins.
- Do not impersonate any person or business, or misrepresent your licences, ratings or affiliation.
- Do not post content that infringes intellectual property, including counterfeit or replica goods.

## 2. Allowed, restricted and blocked categories

Every category has one of three states:

- **Allowed**: anyone can request and any verified seller can quote.
- **Restricted (licensed sellers only)**: buyers can request, but only sellers who upload and pass review of the required licence can see or quote these requests. The licence type, number, issuing body and expiry are stored and re-checked before expiry. Request and quote screens show the regulatory disclaimer for the category.
- **Blocked**: cannot be requested or quoted at all. Our text classifier also catches free-text requests that try to get around this.

When we are unsure whether a category is lawful to offer, it starts as **Blocked**. The current policy for I Want USA is:

| Category | Policy | At launch | Who may quote / licence rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Insurance (health, motor/auto, life, home) | Restricted (licensed sellers only) | **Blocked** until legal sign-off | State-licensed insurance producer (agent or broker) whose licence is valid in the buyer's state, checked against the NIPR producer database. |
| Loans, credit and credit cards | Blocked | Same | Not available (state lending licences and federal consumer credit rules). |
| Investments, securities and crypto-assets | Blocked | Same | Not available (SEC / FINRA rules). |
| Real estate sale and rental | Restricted (licensed sellers only) | Same | Real estate broker or salesperson licence issued by the state where the property is located. |
| Legal services | Restricted (licensed sellers only) | Same | Attorney with active admission to the bar of the state whose law governs the matter; bar number and state shown on the profile. |
| Medical providers, doctors and clinics | Restricted (licensed sellers only) | Same | Provider licensed by the relevant state board for the services quoted. No telehealth prescribing and no prescriptions through the app. |
| Medicines and pharmacy (prescription or OTC) | Blocked | Same | Not available. |
| Alcohol, tobacco, vapes and cannabis | Blocked | Same | Not available. |
| Firearms, ammunition, weapons and weapon parts | Blocked | Same | Not available. |
| Contractors (electrical, plumbing, HVAC, roofing, general) | Restricted (licensed sellers only) | Same | Contractor licence where the buyer's state (or locality) requires one for the job; licence number, classification and expiry shown on the quote. |

## 3. Always prohibited

Regardless of category, you may not request, offer or quote for:

- Weapons, firearms, ammunition, explosives, weapon parts and accessories designed to make weapons more lethal.
- Drugs, controlled substances, drug paraphernalia, prescription and over-the-counter medicines, and cannabis products.
- Alcohol, tobacco, vapes and nicotine products.
- Counterfeit, replica or stolen goods, and goods that infringe a trademark, copyright or patent.
- Adult or sexual services, escort services, and any content sexualizing minors.
- Loans, credit repair, debt settlement, investments, securities, cryptocurrency and money transmission.
- Gambling, lotteries and sweepstakes.
- Human remains, body parts, live animals protected by law, and products made from endangered species.
- Recalled products, hazardous materials and products banned by the Consumer Product Safety Commission.
- Fake documents, identity documents, academic cheating, hacking services, personal data or accounts.
- Services that would require you to break the law, including unlicensed regulated services.

## 4. Regulatory disclaimers

These disclaimers are shown on request and quote screens in the categories they apply to:

- **Insurance (health, motor/auto, life, home):** Insurance quotes are provided by independently licensed insurance producers, not by Calecute Technologies LLC. Calecute Technologies LLC is not an insurance agency or producer and does not sell, solicit or negotiate insurance. Coverage, eligibility and prices are determined by the insurer. Producer licence number: <shown on each quote>.
- **Real estate sale and rental:** Real estate services are provided by independently licensed brokers and agents. All listings and quotes are subject to the federal Fair Housing Act: we do not allow any preference, limitation or discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, handicap, familial status or national origin, or any other class protected by state or local law.
- **Legal services:** ATTORNEY ADVERTISING. Quotes are provided by independent attorneys, not by Calecute Technologies LLC, which is not a law firm, does not provide legal advice and does not refer or recommend lawyers. Sending a request or receiving a quote does not create an attorney-client relationship. Prior results do not guarantee a similar outcome.
- **Medical providers, doctors and clinics:** I Want USA does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment and is not a healthcare provider. Do not share detailed health information in requests or chat. In an emergency call 911.
- **Contractors (electrical, plumbing, HVAC, roofing, general):** Licensing requirements vary by state and job size. Check the contractor's licence with your state licensing board before work starts. Permits may be required.
- **Moving (local and interstate):** Interstate moves are regulated by FMCSA. Ask for the mover's USDOT number and the "Your Rights and Responsibilities When You Move" booklet.
- **Used vehicles:** Check the vehicle history and title before you pay. Used vehicles sold by dealers must display the FTC Buyers Guide.
- **Everyday goods (appliances, electronics, furniture) and home services:** Sales tax may apply. Prices are set by the seller.

## 5. Reviews

Reviews must reflect a real, completed order. No paid, incentivized, swapped or self-written reviews, and no threats to leave a bad review to get a discount. Sellers may reply once, politely.

## 6. Enforcement

You can **report** and **block** any user, request, quote, chat or review. Content with several reports may be hidden automatically while we review it. Uploaded photos are screened automatically for unsafe content. Depending on severity we may remove content, issue a warning, limit features, remove a Verified badge, or suspend or close the account, and we may report illegal content to law enforcement. You can appeal by writing to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

If you see something that puts someone in immediate danger, call 911.
