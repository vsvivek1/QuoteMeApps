# Delete Your I Want USA Account

_Version 1.0.0 · Last updated 2026-10-02_

This page explains how to delete your account in **I Want USA**, the app published by **Calecute Technologies LLC**, and what happens to your data. It works without the app installed.

## Option 1: in the app (fastest)

1. Open I Want USA and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings > Delete account**.
3. Read what will be deleted and kept, then confirm. For your security we ask you to sign in again (one-time code, Google or Apple).
4. Your account is closed immediately and the deletion completes within 30 days.

If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke I Want USA's access to your Apple ID.

## Option 2: without the app

Send a deletion request from this page's form at https://{{USA_WEB_DOMAIN}}/legal/account-deletion, or email **{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}** with the subject "Delete my account" from the email linked to your account, or include your registered phone number. We will send a one-time code to your registered phone number to confirm the request is yours, and confirm when deletion is complete.

## Before you delete

- **Store subscriptions are not cancelled automatically.** Cancel a Google Play or App Store seller plan in the store first. Website plans are cancelled automatically.
- Open requests are closed and open quotes are withdrawn. Sellers you accepted will no longer be able to contact you through the app.
- Unused quote credits and plan time are lost and are not refunded, except as described in the [Refund and Cancellation Policy](/legal/refund-cancellation).

## What is deleted

Within 30 days we delete or irreversibly anonymize:

- Your profile: name, phone number, email, addresses, photo and saved locations
- Requests, quotes, chat messages and uploaded photos and videos
- Seller business profile, business photos and service area
- Notification tokens, device identifiers and preferences
- Analytics data linked to your account ID

Backups containing your data are overwritten within 35 days.

## What is kept, and for how long

Some data must be kept for legal, tax or safety reasons, with access restricted:

- Purchase and invoice records for seller plans and credits (kept 7 years for tax and accounting law)
- Seller verification and licence records (12 months after deletion, for fraud prevention)
- Records of reports, blocks and suspensions linked to your phone number (up to 3 years, to stop banned users from returning)
- Records of the consents you gave and withdrew (3 years)
- Server and security logs (deleted within 90 days)
- Reviews you wrote stay published without your name, so sellers' ratings stay accurate
- Order records held by the other party to a completed order show your details anonymized

## Delete some data without deleting your account

You can delete individual requests, photos and reviews in the app, clear saved addresses in Settings > Addresses, turn off analytics in Settings > Privacy, or email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} to ask us to delete specific data.
