# Children's Privacy (COPPA) Statement

_Version 1.0.0 · Last updated 2026-10-02_

I Want USA is a marketplace for adults to request and quote for products and services. It is **not directed to children under 13**, and you must be at least 18 years old to create an account.

## Our practices

- We do not knowingly collect, use or disclose personal information from children under 13, as defined by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the FTC's COPPA Rule.
- Sign-up includes an age confirmation. Users who indicate they are under 18 cannot create an account.
- The app has no ads, no behavioral advertising and no third-party tracking, and the app's store listings target adults (Google Play target audience: 18 and over).
- Content rules in our [Community Guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines) prohibit any content that sexualizes or endangers minors; such content is removed and reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) as required by law.

## If a child has given us information

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it and close the account promptly, without first seeking parental consent, as permitted by the COPPA Rule. We will not use it for any purpose other than responding to the deletion.

Parents or guardians who believe their child has used I Want USA or provided personal information can contact us at **{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}** (subject "Child privacy") with the account's phone number or email, and we will investigate and delete the data. We will tell you when this is done.

## Teens

Users aged 13 to 17 may not create accounts either. If we learn that a user is under 18, we close the account and delete its data under our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).
