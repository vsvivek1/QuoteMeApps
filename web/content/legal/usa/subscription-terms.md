# Seller Subscription Terms

_Version 1.0.0 · Last updated 2026-10-02_

These terms are shown before you buy a seller plan or quote credits in I Want USA. They supplement the [Seller Terms](/legal/seller-terms) and the [Refund and Cancellation Policy](/legal/refund-cancellation). Buyers never pay to use I Want USA.

## 1. Launch period and founding partners

Seller features are currently free while monetization is off. Sellers who join before monetization starts are founding partners and keep free access until **{{EARLY_PARTNER_FREE_UNTIL}}**. You will not be charged unless you choose a plan, and we will tell you at least 30 days before any free access you have ends.

## 2. What you can buy

| Product | Billing | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Seller plan, monthly | Every month, auto-renewing | Higher or unlimited monthly quote limit, Verified-first priority window on new requests, featured placement labelled "Sponsored", seller analytics |
| Seller plan, annual | Every 12 months, auto-renewing | Same as monthly, at a lower effective monthly price |
| Quote-credit pack | One-time purchase | A fixed number of extra quotes, valid for 12 months |

Without a plan, sellers get 5 free quotes per month once monetization starts. The exact price, currency (USD), billing period and any sales tax are shown on the purchase screen before you confirm. Prices can differ between Google Play, the App Store and our website.

## 3. Free trials and introductory offers

If a free trial or introductory price is offered, the purchase screen shows its length and the price you will pay when it ends. Unless you cancel before the trial or introductory period ends, the subscription converts to the regular price shown. Each seller account is eligible for one trial. We may also offer upgrade discounts, offer codes and win-back offers; their terms are shown with the offer.

## 4. Auto-renewal

- **App Store:** Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage and cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings after purchase: https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions
- **Google Play:** The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period until cancelled. You can cancel, pause or resubscribe at any time in the Google Play subscription center: https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions?package=com.calecute.iwant.usa
- **Website (Stripe):** Your card is charged at the start of each period until you cancel in the seller dashboard at https://{{USA_SELLER_DASHBOARD_DOMAIN}}. We email a receipt for each charge and remind you before an annual plan renews.

## 5. How to cancel

Cancel in the store or dashboard where you subscribed (links above, or Settings > Seller plan > Manage subscription in the app). Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period, and you keep your benefits until then. Deleting the app does not cancel a store subscription.

## 6. Failed payments, grace period and account hold

If a renewal payment fails, we keep your plan active during the store's grace period and show a "Fix your payment" banner. If payment is not fixed, Google Play may place the subscription on account hold and your plan benefits pause until payment succeeds.

## 7. Price changes

We will tell you before any price increase takes effect. For store subscriptions, Google Play and Apple will notify you and, where their rules require, ask for your consent; if you do not agree, the subscription ends at the end of the current period.

## 8. Restore purchases

If you reinstall the app or switch devices, use **Settings > Seller plan > Restore purchases**. Your plan is linked to your I Want USA account, so a plan bought on any platform works on all of them.
