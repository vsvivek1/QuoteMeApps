# Consent Notice under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

This notice is shown to you before you create an {{APP_NAME}} account and whenever we ask for a new consent. It is given by **{{COMPANY_NAME}}** (CIN {{CIN}}), {{ADDRESS}}, the **Data Fiduciary** for your personal data. It is available in English and Hindi; tap **हिन्दी** at the top of the screen to read it in Hindi. Our full [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy) gives more detail.

## 1. What we ask you to agree to

You can give or refuse each consent separately. Only consent A is needed to use the app.

### A. Account and marketplace (required)

| Personal data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Name, +91 mobile number, email (if you sign in with Google or Apple) | Create and secure your account, sign you in, send service messages |
| Delivery or service address, PIN code, and GPS location when you choose "Use my location" | Match your request to nearby sellers; show sellers only your locality until you accept a quote |
| Requests (text, photos, budget, dates), quotes, chat messages, reviews | Run the marketplace: send your request to matching sellers, show quotes, chat, orders and reviews |
| Your phone number and exact address, shared only with the seller whose quote you accept | Let that seller deliver or provide the service |
| Seller details: business name, GSTIN, Udyam number, shop photos, registrations and licences | Verify sellers and check registrations for restricted categories |
| Purchase records for seller plans and credits | Grant plan benefits, issue GST invoices |
| Device and push notification IDs, crash and diagnostic data | Send notifications, keep the app working, prevent fraud and abuse |

### B. Usage analytics (optional, off by default)

| Personal data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| App interactions (screens viewed, features used) linked to a pseudonymous ID | Understand how the app is used and improve it (Firebase Analytics) |

### C. Offers and updates (optional, off by default)

| Personal data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Mobile number, email, push token | Send offers, tips and product news by push notification, email, SMS or WhatsApp (SMS and WhatsApp only through DLT-registered templates) |

Location, camera, photos, microphone (voice input) and notifications also need your device permission, which you can refuse or withdraw in your phone's settings at any time.

## 2. Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw any consent at any time, as easily as you gave it:

- In the app: **Settings > Privacy > Manage consent** (switch B or C off, or withdraw A by deleting your account).
- By email to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}.

After you withdraw, we stop processing the data for that purpose within a reasonable time and erase it unless the law requires us to keep it (for example GST invoices). Withdrawal does not affect processing done before it. If you withdraw consent A, we will close your account because we cannot run the marketplace for you without it.

## 3. Your rights

You have the right to access a summary of your personal data, to correction, completion, updating and erasure, to grievance redressal, and to nominate a person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity. Use **Settings > Privacy**, email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}, or contact our Grievance Officer.

When you tap **Agree**, we record which consents you gave, the version of this notice and the [Terms of Use](/legal/terms-of-service) and [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy) you accepted, and the date and time. You can see your current choices in Settings > Privacy > Manage consent.
