# Refund and Cancellation Policy

This policy covers payments made **to {{COMPANY_NAME}}** for {{APP_NAME}} seller subscriptions and quote-credit packs. Buyers use {{APP_NAME}} for free.

**Purchases between buyers and sellers are not covered.** Buyers pay sellers directly, outside the Service, so refunds, returns, cancellations and warranty claims for goods or services must be agreed with the seller under the seller's own policy and the law. You can report a seller who refuses to honor an accepted quote, and we may act against the seller under our [Community Guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines).

## 1. Where you bought determines who handles it

| Where you paid | How to cancel | How to ask for a refund |
|---|---|---|
| Google Play (Android app) | Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, or the "Manage subscription" link in the app | Google handles refunds under the Google Play refund policy. Within 48 hours of purchase you can request one at play.google.com; after that, contact us and we will review it under Section 3 |
| Apple App Store (iOS app) | iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions, or the "Manage subscription" link in the app | Apple handles all refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds for App Store purchases |
| Our website (seller dashboard, paid with {{PAYMENT_PROVIDER}}) | Seller dashboard > Billing > Cancel plan, or the {{PAYMENT_PROVIDER}} customer portal link in your receipt | Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}; we review it under Section 3 |

## 2. Cancelling a subscription

- You can cancel at any time. Cancellation stops the next renewal; you keep your plan benefits until the end of the period you already paid for.
- Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription. Cancel it in the store first. Deleting your {{APP_NAME}} account cancels website subscriptions automatically.
- If you cancel during a free trial, you will not be charged. Apple and Google may require you to cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

## 3. Refunds for website purchases

- **Subscriptions.** Payments for a started billing period are non-refundable, except where required by law, for duplicate or mistaken charges, or if the Service was unavailable for a significant part of the period because of a fault on our side. We do not give partial refunds for unused time after cancellation.
- **Annual plans.** If you cancel an annual plan within 14 days of the initial purchase (not a renewal), we refund it in full.
- **Quote-credit packs.** Unused credit packs can be refunded within 14 days of purchase if no credits from the pack have been used. Credits expire 12 months after purchase.
- **Account suspension.** If we close your seller account because you broke our Terms, fees already paid are not refunded. If we close it without cause, we refund the unused part of any prepaid period.
- **Disputed charges.** Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank so we can help.

## 4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds for website purchases go back to the original payment method through {{PAYMENT_PROVIDER}}, usually within 5 to 10 business days of approval, depending on your bank. Any unused credits or plan benefits linked to the refunded purchase are removed.
