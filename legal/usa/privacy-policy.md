# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how {{COMPANY_NAME}} ("{{COMPANY_NAME}}", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use the {{APP_NAME}} mobile app for Android and iOS, the website at https://{{WEB_DOMAIN}} and the seller dashboard at https://{{SELLER_DASHBOARD_DOMAIN}} (together, the "Service").

{{APP_NAME}} is a reverse marketplace for the {{COUNTRY_NAME}}: buyers post what they want and independent sellers send quotes. {{APP_NAME}} is a separate app from "I Want India"; the two apps share no accounts and no data.

If you are a California resident, also read our [California Privacy Notice](/legal/ccpa-notice), which forms part of this policy. Our [Children's Privacy (COPPA) Statement](/legal/coppa) explains why the Service is not for children.

## 1. Summary

- We collect what we need to run the marketplace: your account and phone number, the requests and quotes you post, chat messages, photos you upload, the location you choose for a request, and basic device, crash and usage data.
- We **do not sell** your personal information and we **do not share** it for cross-context behavioral advertising. The app shows **no ads**, does **not track** you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not use the Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA.
- Your data is stored by our database provider Supabase in **{{DATA_REGION}}**.
- A buyer's exact address and phone number are shown to a seller **only after the buyer accepts that seller's quote**.
- You can delete your account at any time in **Settings > Delete account** or at [https://{{WEB_DOMAIN}}/legal/account-deletion](/legal/account-deletion).

## 3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

1. Create and secure your account and sign you in (phone one-time code, Google or Apple).
2. Run the marketplace: post requests, match them to sellers by category and service area, deliver quotes, chat, accept quotes, track orders and publish reviews.
3. Verify sellers, check licences for restricted categories and show "Verified" badges.
4. Send notifications you asked for (new quotes, messages, order updates) and service messages. Marketing messages are sent only if you opt in, and you can opt out at any time.
5. Keep the Service safe: detect fake or duplicate requests, spam, scams and abuse; enforce our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service) and [Community Guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines); handle reports and blocks; moderate uploaded photos.
6. Process seller plan and quote-credit purchases and keep accounting and tax records.
7. Analyze aggregated usage and fix crashes and bugs.
8. Comply with law and respond to lawful requests.

We do not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects. Seller ranking in quote lists is explained in our [Seller Terms](/legal/seller-terms).

## 4. What other users see

- **Sellers matching your request** see its category, description, photos, quantity, needed-by date, your budget (unless you hide it), the locality or ZIP code (not the exact address) and your display name.
- **After you accept a quote**, that seller also sees your exact address and phone number so they can deliver or provide the service. Before acceptance, contact happens only through in-app chat.
- **Seller profiles** (business name, photos, description, categories, service area, rating, reviews, verification badge and licence numbers for restricted categories) are public.
- **Reviews** you write are public with your display name.

## 7. Your choices and rights

All users can:

- **Access and download** your data: Settings > Privacy > Download my data, or email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}.
- **Correct** your profile in the app, or ask us to correct other data.
- **Delete** your account and data: Settings > Delete account, or the [Account Deletion page](/legal/account-deletion).
- **Turn off analytics** in Settings > Privacy, **turn off notifications** in Settings > Notifications or your device settings, and **withdraw location, camera, photo or microphone permission** in your device settings.
- **Opt out of marketing** using the unsubscribe link or Settings > Notifications.

Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Iowa, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Texas, Utah, Virginia and other states with consumer privacy laws have the rights those laws give, which may include the rights to know, access, correct, delete, obtain a portable copy, opt out of sale, targeted advertising and profiling, and to appeal our decision. We do not sell personal information, do not process it for targeted advertising, and do not profile in furtherance of decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. To exercise a right, email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} or use the in-app options above. We will verify your request by confirming control of your account phone number or email, and respond within 45 days (extendable once by 45 days where the law allows). If we decline your request you may appeal by replying to our decision with the subject "Appeal"; we will answer within the period the law requires, and if you are not satisfied you may contact your state Attorney General. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal on our websites as an opt-out of sale and sharing.

## 8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our hosting provider. Access is controlled by row-level security rules so users can only read the data they are allowed to see; verification documents are stored in private storage. Staff access is limited and logged. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal information we will notify you and regulators as the law requires.
